Spikeroll

Spikeroll posts short clips of GTA 6 streams. Every clip credits its streamer.

Privacy policy

The Spikeroll clipper is a private tool. It is used only by its owner, to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube API Services. It does not collect, store or share personal data from anyone else.

It uses YouTube API Services: see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
